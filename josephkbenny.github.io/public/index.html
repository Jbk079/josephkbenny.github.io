Research publication

Journal paper · M.Tech thesis · Aug 2024

Optimizing Question-Answering Systems with T5 Transformer and Focus Generation Modules

Joseph Benny, Neetha Joseph, Dr. Elizabeth Isaac, Dr. Surekha Mariam Varghese

High Technology Letters, Vol. 30, Issue 8, August 2024, pp. 473–485 · ISSN 1006-6748

DOI 10.37896/HTL30.8/11443

My M.Tech research, published as first author. I fine-tuned T5 transformers to generate question and answer pairs from course material, and added a focus module that grades each question as easy, medium, hard or expert. A cosine-similarity check removes duplicate questions and answers. The model was trained on Natural Questions, SQuAD and C4, then deployed as a web app that helps faculty prepare viva assessments.

  1. Input passage
  2. T5 question generation
  3. Focus mode: 4 difficulty levels
  4. Similarity check
  5. T5 answer generation
  6. Graded QA pairs
